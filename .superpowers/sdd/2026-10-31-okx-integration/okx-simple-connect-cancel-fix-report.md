- Each pending submit has its own immutable request, observation/identity/environment, digest of all three captured credential fields, and independent UTC plus monotonic 120-second budget. Only the exact successful protected Windows result can approve that request; tests call the internal seam explicitly and do not establish OS approval.
- Encryption, private staging creation, ciphertext flush and exact decrypt/readback happen before acquiring the controller's short revocation lock. Staging is ciphertext only and is never enumerated as an account.
- Final admission and a single no-replace native handle rename share the controller `sync` lock with STOP/emergency/generation changes. A registered cancellation callback takes the same lock, covering native Anuluj/window close, form lifetime/timeout, caller cancellation and controller lifetime. The winner is the first serialized revocation or successful commit, not the earlier status update. Cancellation requested during an admitted rename waits for that serialized operation, then disconnects the saved account; cancellation cannot roll back a completed commit.
- At final admission recheck exact approval/request/observation/all-field binding, generation/current request, token/revocation and both clocks (including rollback). The final file and saved-account projection become visible only on successful rename. A later canceled form returns the saved account with disconnected status, never 'nothing saved'.
- Owned staging uses an exclusive writable native handle with delete/rename rights; cancellation cleanup marks that exact handle for deletion, not an arbitrary path. Unknown prior staging/final files are preserved and refused, never repaired, overwritten or promoted on restart. Abrupt process interruption may leave encrypted unexplained staging, which is preserved/refused, not future enrollment.
